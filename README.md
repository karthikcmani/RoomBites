# RoomBites

A lightweight hostel-exclusive food ordering and management platform.

## Vision

RoomBites is a student-to-student food ordering platform, initially designed
for hostel communities at Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Technology (RIT), Kottayam.

Built by students, for students.

## Project Status

Currently in Phase 0: Product Definition and Architecture.

## Planned Features

- Student authentication
- Product and menu management
- Shopping cart
- Real-time order management
- Admin dashboard
- Customer online presence
- Cash and UPI payments
- Credit management
- Order tracking
- Notifications
- Multi-platform support
